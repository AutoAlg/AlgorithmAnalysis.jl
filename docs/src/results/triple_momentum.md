# Triple Momentum
 For the triple momentum method applied to L-smooth and m-strongly convex functions, the distance to optimality converges at a rate of 1-1/√κ where κ = L/m.
## References
- Bryan Van Scoy, Randy A. Freeman and Kevin M. Lynch. *The Fastest Known Globally Convergent First-Order Method for Minimizing Strongly Convex Functions*. 2018. [doi:10.1109/LCSYS.2017.2722406](https://doi.org/10.1109/LCSYS.2017.2722406)
